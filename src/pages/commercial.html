Designed for commercial environments

A cost-effective alternative to refurbishment

Commercial vinyl wrapping is ideal for hotels, retail spaces, offices, student accommodation and healthcare settings. Our thermoformable architectural vinyl can be applied to most smooth surfaces, including wood, aluminium and PVC, allowing walls, furniture and fixtures to be refreshed without removal or replacement.

Built for busy spaces, our films are resistant to heat, humidity and everyday wear, easy to clean, and available in hundreds of finishes. By reusing existing surfaces you get a fresh, professional look at a fraction of the cost, reduce waste, and keep operations running.
