Good to know

Questions people ask before booking

How much does vinyl wrapping cost?

Every project is priced individually, but as a rule wrapping works out at 50–70% less than replacing the same units. A typical kitchen is a fraction of the cost of a new one, with no builders, plumbers or electricians needed. Send us photos and a rough door count and we'll give you a clear price, usually within one working day.

How long does it take?

Most kitchens are completed in two to three days, wardrobes and single pieces of furniture often in a day. Commercial projects are scheduled around your opening hours to keep downtime to a minimum. Your kitchen stays usable throughout.

What can be wrapped?

Almost any smooth, sound surface: kitchen doors, drawer fronts and end panels, worktops, wardrobes, doors, walls, reception desks, lift interiors, cubicles, lockers and shop fittings. Laminate, MDF, wood, aluminium and PVC all take vinyl well. If you're not sure, send a photo and we'll tell you.

How long does it last, and is it guaranteed?

We only fit premium architectural films such as 3M DI-NOC and Cover Styl', which are designed for busy kitchens and commercial spaces. They're resistant to heat, humidity and everyday wear, and every installation comes with a 10-year guarantee against cracking, yellowing and delamination.

What colours and finishes are there?

Hundreds. Matt and gloss solid colours, wood grains, marble and stone effects, concrete, metallics and textured fabrics. We bring physical samples to your home or site visit so you can see them in your own light.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Liphook, Hampshire and work across Surrey, Hampshire, Berkshire, West Sussex and London, including Guildford, Woking, Basingstoke, Farnham, Windsor and surrounding areas.
